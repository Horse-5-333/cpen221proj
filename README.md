### Brief Problem Statement
Engineering programs come with a heavy load of assignments, often with conflicting deadlines. At UBC, this is made worse by due dates and exam information being scattered across multiple platforms, with students forced to frequently check multiple platforms out of fear of missing vital information. Keeping track of dates becomes its own task before students have even started the work itself. As most courses assign weekly homework to be completed on Sunday at midnight, knowing how to spread out coursework throughout the week becomes difficult, especially since assignments often rely on content learned that week. Added to the work of completing projects and studying for quizzes, tests, and exams, students are left overwhelmed by logistics. For many, the result is failed courses, burnout, or, in extreme cases, leaving the program altogether.

Beyond managing deadlines, help for coursework can be hard to find for students without established study groups. The most common option for students at UBC is Piazza, which does not encourage open conversation and has slow or nonexistent responses, especially near deadlines. On Piazza, peer feedback on assignments or writing in subjective courses like English is also impossible without posting the entire assignment for the whole class to read. Office hours are short and infrequent, and often professors disallow direct help with solving assignments. Students who would rather study in a group setting face challenges in finding peers to study with and rooms to study in. As a result, many students struggle through assignments alone and cannot get the help they need to succeed. 
### Brief Solution Statement
We envision a mobile-focused web app that gives each UBC course a shared calendar of assignment deadlines, exams and other important dates, kept up to date by students submitting updates. Each course page also has a community board where students can ask questions, form teams and organize group study sessions. See SPECIFICATION.md for the full specification.
