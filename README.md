### Brief Problem Statement
Engineering programs are infamous for the amount of assignments and deadlines, often occurring on the same day. However, at UBC, information for when assignments are due and exams happen is scattered across multiple platforms, leading to anxiety and confusion. Keeping up with assignment deadlines and time management of said assignments becomes its own task, let alone completing the assignments themselves. Additionally, when doing assignments, help can be hard to find for students who don’t have established groups, as platforms like Piazza do not encourage open conversation and responses may be slow or not come at all, especially near the assignment deadline. Courses where correctness is not entirely clear or is subjective (e.g. English) are near-impossible to study for without peer review, which is currently left completely to the student to find friends or peers to study with without a clear pathway to find them.
